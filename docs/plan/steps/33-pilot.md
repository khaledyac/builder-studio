# 33 · Pilot with the Retail domain

**Milestone:** M5 · **Tag:** ready-for-human · **Size:** M · **Blocked by:** 30, 32

## Why

Learn from real builders before opening more domains. Architecture: §13, §14.

## Plan

- **Who and how long:** 10–20 Retail builders, for 4–6 weeks.
- **Support:** an onboarding session, weekly office hours, and a feedback button in the UI.
- **Success metrics:**
  - apps published per builder,
  - time from idea to Lab,
  - share of changes that take the fast path,
  - turnaround of Tier 2 approvals,
  - builder satisfaction,
  - cost per builder against the estimate (about $72 a month at pilot scale),
  - incidents.
- **Feedback loop:** a weekly review. Tune the fast-path limits and the workspace size (the open points in §14). Decide on the next domain.

## Done when

The go or no-go decision for the next domain is documented, with the metrics behind it.
